# Keep audio supportive and test systems at the right level

Audio scope is a soft ambient loop, short UI, tool, catch, sell, and donate cues, plus a mute toggle; no required information may be audio-only. Pure systems such as Pocket rules, recipes, donations, loan progress, task completion, save/load, and economy should be unit-tested, while Core Play Smoke should be browser-tested with nonblank rendering and console checks.

## Considered Options

- Supportive audio plus unit tests for pure systems and browser smoke for integrated play.
- Larger music system and broad browser-only validation.
- Syntax and build checks only.

## Consequences

Testing must prove both the arithmetic of the island loop and the lived browser path. Build success alone is not evidence that the clone works as a playable island.
