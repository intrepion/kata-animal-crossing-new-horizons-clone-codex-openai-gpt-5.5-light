# Use forgiving collision and desktop-first controls

The island will use Forgiving Collision based on simple circles or boxes with generous interaction radii, not mesh-perfect collision or a full physics simulation. Delivery is desktop-first with responsive layout and basic touch affordances only when cheap, preserving the third-person keyboard-and-camera feel as the primary experience.

## Considered Options

- Shape-based forgiving collision and desktop-first controls.
- Precise mesh collision.
- Mobile-first touch controls.

## Consequences

Collision should support cozy movement and clear interaction targeting, not technical realism. Touch controls may exist as a convenience, but they should not weaken the desktop camera, movement, tool wheel, or keyboard-only path.
