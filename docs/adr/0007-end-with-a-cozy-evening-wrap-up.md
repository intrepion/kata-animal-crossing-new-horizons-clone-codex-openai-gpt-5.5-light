# End with a cozy evening wrap-up

The Island Day completes when the player repays the Starter Loan milestone after donating at least one creature, crafting at least two tools or items, and completing a small set of NookPhone Tasks. The ending should be an Evening Wrap-Up rather than a hard win screen, reinforcing that the island continues even though the slice is complete.

## Considered Options

- Starter Loan milestone plus proof of donation, crafting, and task progress.
- Pure loan repayment.
- Open-ended play with no completion moment.

## Consequences

The completion path must touch the main systems, so final verification can prove the clone is more than movement and menus. The ending should feel like the close of a satisfying first day, not an arcade victory state.
