# Pace economy for one focused session

The Starter Loan should be repayable in roughly 10-15 minutes of ordinary play, using branches, weeds, softwood, stone, clay, shells, flowers, and creature specimens as the initial material set. This makes the Island Day prove the full sell, donate, craft, and repay loop without turning Bells into either a trivial score or a long grind.

## Considered Options

- Starter Loan repayable in one focused 10-15 minute session.
- Very short repayment where Bells barely matter.
- Longer repayment that pushes completion beyond the slice.

## Consequences

Balance should encourage the player to gather, catch, sell, donate, and craft in the same session. The resource list should stay small enough to learn by sight but broad enough to make different Landmarks feel materially distinct.
