# Support placeables and accessibility baseline

The island will support a small number of Placeables with persistence and light collision, and it will meet an Accessibility Baseline from day one: keyboard-only play, documented keys, reduced-motion support for camera sway or transitions, readable contrast, and no audio-only cues. Making the island yours needs visible saved world state, and cozy play still needs crisp usability.

## Considered Options

- Persistent Placeables plus accessibility baseline.
- Cosmetic-only placed items.
- Accessibility improvements deferred until after core mechanics.

## Consequences

Placeables should be limited enough to keep collision and persistence simple. Controls, UI, prompts, and feedback should be designed with keyboard and reduced-motion play in mind rather than patched after implementation.
